Use this library as 25 pages of 4 prompt slots. Each prompt is written for beatless sustained ambient modulation.

## 001. Low Voltage Fog

- id: `001_low_voltage_fog`
- category: `analog_drone`
- controls: cfg_musiccoca=7.4, cfg_notes=6.5, temperature=0.58, top_k=36

Create an original beatless ambient synthesizer texture called Low Voltage Fog. A continuous low-register detuned modular VCO stack low analog drone with slow phase beating and continuous harmonic or noise movement. Add warm saturation, tape delay smear, and a stable plate reverb tail with a wide stable stereo field. The sound should feel like heavy fog and evolve continuously as a sustained environment, not a beat, song section, or lead melody. Avoid drums, percussion, vocals, arpeggios, sequencer patterns, rhythmic pulses, EDM drops, sudden cuts, fade-outs, and silence.
